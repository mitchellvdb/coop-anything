Starting point: Claude Code's be01477, including test83 diagnostics filtering,
protocol37 manual formation, player names/connected count and test85 native
provisions Back/Escape. Earlier contributions are retained.

Implemented changes:
- Unknown party-owner labels clear their output; real GL fixture begins with a
  nonzero buffer. Post-combat manual reorder preserves the shared baseline until
  native animation settles, then sends its final change once.
- Stagecoach retries missing startup probes and excludes independently viewed/new
  quirk flags from gameplay fingerprints. New component traces diagnose true
  post-week or post-upgrade differences. Exact same-member verification remains.
- All players can move Hamlet trinkets using logical item identity even if the
  host estate order differs from a requesting guest. Building-assigned living
  heroes are eligible; native class/duplicate-slot restrictions remain.
- A known raid loot/bag presentation wrapper may hold an Item copy rather than
  the canonical pointer. Resolve one exact type/id/quantity match in that owning
  inventory only; unrelated wrappers or multiple matching items refuse. Native
  cancellation and ordered move remain. Live direct loot equipment is pending.
- Combat item authorization checks the active hero's owner, preserving the native
  nullable or teammate recipient. Four-role fixture effects are modeled. Native
  Dog Treat buff/quantity needs live confirmation.
- Inventory-full OK resolves the captured manager's front tracked informational
  entry when its callback is dispatched outside rendering. Exact empty callback,
  name, causal loot sequence and area remain required; native removal is deferred
  once through ShowEntry. Earlier fixture dispatched only during rendering.
- Local genuine desync notifies the attached launcher immediately as well as
  leaving a log marker; normal connection loss creates no immediate event.
- Guest Take control, living roster filtering and LocalAppData guest report path
  are isolated launcher changes documented in OWNERSHIP/DIAGNOSTICS.
