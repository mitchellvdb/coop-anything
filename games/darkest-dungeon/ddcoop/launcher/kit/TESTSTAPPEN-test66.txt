DDCoop test66 - inventory stacks and automatic resync
==================================================

1. Close all old game windows and let guest recovery finish. Install test66 on
   EVERY PC. The launcher protocol changed: do not mix test65 and test66.
2. Select Players 4, Host, and let all three guests join before loading. Check
   Player1..4 assignments. Keep every DDCoop launcher open throughout the test.
3. Buy supplies from all players. Take at least two food stacks if practical.
   Compare counts, gold and stock. Provision-stack returns remain unsupported.
4. Embark, play a fight/loot, move and split ordinary inventory stacks, eat food,
   then change rooms. Compare both stacks on all four copies. A repair must not
   alter an already repaired stack or stop at its final hash.
5. In a safe room, have the host click Resync... and confirm. Finish any normal
   game exit confirmation. Leave every launcher open and wait: games close,
   guest saves recover, the fresh host save transfers, then all games reopen
   and load together automatically. Do NOT click Host/Join or start Steam games.
6. Compare the restored room/checkpoint, party, inventory and hero owners. The
   exact unsaved moment is not guaranteed. Player numbers must remain unchanged.
7. If successful, test Resync a second time and continue to another room/fight.
8. Copy logs from EVERY PC, also after success. If a game crashes, collect its
   launcher/runtime/app logs before more sessions overwrite them. New Fault trace
   entries help locate the earlier provisions crash; no memory dump is collected.

Automatic resync needs every selected player's launcher still connected. If one
has left or recovery fails, DDCoop stops; let recovery finish and start a fresh
manual Host/Join session. It never force-kills a game or ignores a failed restore.
Solo .bat guests have no launcher; their restart remains manual.
Guests always use -nosave. The host keeps its save-progress preference.
New behavior has automated coverage; live four-PC testing is still required.
