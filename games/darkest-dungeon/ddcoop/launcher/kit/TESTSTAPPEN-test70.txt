DDCoop test70 - independent recheck and campaign-DLC groundwork
==============================================================

Install the SAME test70 kit on every PC after closing old game windows and
letting guest recovery finish. Guests remain -nosave; host saving follows its
checkbox. Open the launcher and connect the selected number of players.

1. Try walking from each guest while the others stand still. Open/cancel a
   curio, use food, play a fight and compare inventory, HP and hero control.
2. If camping is available, use a learned/selected camping skill. No unexpected
   skill or actor should be used. Complete the mission and advance results
   together, then compose the next party and embark again.
3. Copy logs immediately after any stop or error. Queue/transport failures and
   a known town mismatch now stop all shared actions; do not continue privately.
4. With a DLC campaign, confirm the launcher accepts the matching installed
   feature data. Missing/corrupt campaign metadata and missing/empty required
   DLC files are refused before guest backup/replacement.
