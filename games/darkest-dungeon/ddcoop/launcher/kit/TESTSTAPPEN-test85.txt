DDCoop test85 - test84 plus a fix for Back/Escape in the provisions screen

Close game windows before installing. Host installs test85; friends on test77
or newer can accept its signed update when joining. Everyone uses the same kit.
No solo/duo kit for test85 (duo61 stays the solo kit).

1. Provisions screen (the shop before leaving on a mission), with 3-4 players:
   - A guest presses Back. On every other screen the shop slides away by
     itself within a few seconds and everyone is back in town. Nobody has to
     press Back again, and the queue does not get stuck.
   - Repeat with the host pressing Back.
   - Press Escape twice quickly on one PC. That player must not end up with a
     black screen; everyone returns to town.
   - While one player's Back is on its way, press Back on another PC too.
   - Open the shop again afterwards, buy something, and leave on the mission:
     gold and supplies must match on every screen.
2. Everything from test84 still applies: names in the launcher and in the raid
   blocks, "x/4 players connected", and a hero dragged to another rank outside
   a fight moves on every screen.
3. Trinkets: if an equipped trinket seems to disappear, note which hero, which
   trinket, on whose screen and right after which stop, and send the report.

Names are shown to the other players and in your own launcher only; they are
not written to the logs. Do not post reports publicly.
