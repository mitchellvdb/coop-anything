DDCoop test72 - combat to loot transition
=========================================

Close old games, let guest recovery finish, install the SAME72 on every PC.
Do not mix with71: selection now carries persistent hero identity in the same
wire format. Guest always-nosave; host save checkbox remains unchanged.

1. Recheck one Tavern/Abbey draft before Accept and guest provisioning/embark.
   These working71 features are retained.
2. Play a fight through victory and loot. Finish a fight while controlling the
   host window, then another while controlling a guest. Every copy should leave
   combat and keep responding without a sync-stop. Take/close loot as usual;
   compare party, inventories and selected hero, then continue into another fight.
3. With more guests, repeat with another guest controlling the final action.
   Continue normal play; no artificial delay or manual follow click is required.
4. Copy logs after success or immediately after any stop. Include the host and
   every guest runtime log. Do not continue privately after a shared stop.

The host keeps its game/launcher running. Duo batch guests restart manually;
recovery resumes a host checkpoint and may lose unsaved progress. New native
rendering/integration still requires live testing. This kit repairs the observed
selection-transition timing issue; it does not claim full DLC, exact full-state
verification or repairs for the other open issues. See SELECTION-TRANSITION.md.
