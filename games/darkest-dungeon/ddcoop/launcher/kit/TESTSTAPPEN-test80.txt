DDCoop test80 - saves, local tutorial popups, curio double clicks, skill logs
Only the host installs test80 by hand. Friends with test77, test78 or test79
just join: their launcher asks "Update now?"; after Yes and the install, open
DDCoop and join again. A friend on test76 or older installs test80 by hand.

0. Update: on the friend's PC DDCoop-launcher.log shows "host offers DDCoop
   0.5.0-test80 ... receiving" and "verified"; after Yes the install window
   says "DDCoop updated". Send both launchers' logs if anything fails.
1. Tavern/Abbey saved: put two heroes in the Tavern or Abbey (one from the
   host, one from a guest), then close every game and start a new session.
   Both heroes must still be in their places and the gold spent only once.
   Remove one with the X, restart again: that hero stays free.
2. Skills: on a guest, open one of your own heroes with more than four learned
   skills and click a skill on and off. The host's copy must follow. If nothing
   happens on the guest, send the guest's logs at once: they now say why.
   Also choose skills on the host, restart, and check they are kept.
2a. Tutorials: when a tutorial popup appears on any PC, that player closes it
   with its own button; nobody else needs to. Walking waits until it is closed.
2b. Curios: click an open curio a second time on a guest; nothing extra may
   happen, and the curio and its loot must be the same on every PC.
1a. After every raid: if a synchronization notice appears, send the logs from
   every launcher. They contain "Town: wallet here ..." with each currency.

3. Close every game before installing. Use the usual Install DDCoop.bat.
4. Host or join the same campaign. Wait for the initial candidate comparison.
   Compare Stagecoach names/classes/skills/quirks on every copy. Recruit once
   from a guest and once from the host; the same heroes must join every roster.
5. Finish a normal mission together. Return to the Hamlet, close the reward
   screens and wait for the comparison to finish.
6. If candidates differ or a synchronization notice appears, stop and Copy logs
   from every launcher. Use the host's Resync with all launchers kept open.
   Never recruit different heroes to work around a refusal.

Campaign mods: a listed mod must be present with the same files on every PC.
Missing/different/unknown or unreadable mod data is refused before the guest's
save is replaced. No mods are downloaded or enabled automatically. This file
comparison does not establish gameplay compatibility for arbitrary mods or DLC.
Use an otherwise disposable test campaign for any optional mod comparison test.

Guests always use -nosave; host saving follows its checkbox. Backups/restore
borrow only the selected slot1..9; profile_9 is forbidden. Checkpoint Resync may
lose unsaved progress. The host game and launcher stay running, without manual
host approval for shared choices. Solo batch guests still restart manually.
